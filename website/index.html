Will my product really look exactly right?

Yes. That's the whole point. lumnq locks your product's shape, label text and colors from your photo, so every frame shows the real thing.

What do I need to get started?

One clean product photo. A white-background packshot works best, but a good phone photo works too.

Can I use the videos in paid ads?

Yes. Paid plans include full commercial rights, with presets for Meta, TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

What's a credit?

One credit is roughly one short video render. Longer or higher-resolution outputs use more.
